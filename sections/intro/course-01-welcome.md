# Welcome To The Course

This course develops the official Measuring your Platform objectives for Certified Cloud Native Platform Engineering Associate (CNPA). The certification uses a multiple-choice examination. The course is independently authored and does not claim to simulate the examination.

Each section starts with its own introduction and learning outcomes. Each module provides goals, developed teaching, a playground, a graded lab and a final summary. Each section closes with a capstone lab and a knowledge check, and a final knowledge check closes the course.

The labs use data stored in a kind cluster. You compute completion rates, percentiles and the five DORA delivery metrics with `kubectl` and `jq`, and you write a report that a grader recomputes from the live data. Technical pages remain draft for review.

Read the explanations and worked examples to identify the definition, the data and the limits of each claim, then practise on the data.
