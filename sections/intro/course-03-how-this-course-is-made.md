# How This Course Is Made

Authors map sections to the official Certified Cloud Native Platform Engineering Associate (CNPA) objectives and check definitions against the DORA and developer-productivity research. They review examples for the data used, the denominators and meaningful failure cases.

Structural checks verify reading order, section introductions, closing summaries, draft metadata, lab configuration and YAML question contracts. The labs and playgrounds are built without a test run: only static checks were made, and no runtime execution is claimed.

The [contributors](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS082/graphs/contributors) maintain the material. To [report a mistake](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS082/issues), identify the page and disputed statement. The [license](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS082/blob/main/LICENSE) governs reuse.

Objectives belong to their respective owners. Teaching and knowledge checks are original practice material. Application and service names are illustrative and have no special technical meaning.
