# Interpreting DORA Metrics For Platform Initiatives

Computing the metrics is one skill and reading them correctly is another. This module covers how the model developed and what its current form says, why the metrics belong to a service and not to an organisation, and why a change in the numbers after a platform change does not prove that the platform caused it. The hands-on part judges a claim from planning against data for three services.

## Learning Goals

- Explain how the four keys became five metrics and why MTTR was renamed.
- Read throughput and instability together and recognise a service that got faster and less stable.
- Explain why metrics are reported for each service and why one blended score hides differences.
- Name the limits of a before-and-after comparison and use a service without the change as a comparison.
- Explain the effect of Goodhart's law on delivery metrics used as targets.
- Write a claim about a platform initiative that matches the evidence.

## Before You Start

You should know the five metrics and how they are computed. The playground holds metric values for three services in a ConfigMap. Technical terms are defined where they first appear.
